# Micro Flamma: 60-second demo script

For the team's pitch and demo video. Record on a real computer (screen recorder, 1080p),
on the live site: https://kraukendag.github.io/Nasa_app/. Every number below is shown on the site.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Home**. Click **Strike the match** | "Spacecraft materials are approved by burning them in normal gravity. Watch what gravity does to a flame." |
| 0:07 | Click **Gravity off (0 g)** | "Without gravity, hot gas doesn't rise. The flame turns small, round and blue. NASA's records show the Earth test can be wrong both ways." |
| 0:15 | Scroll to **The evidence**: silicone chart | "Silicone burned completely on Earth but barely at all in space: four SAFFIRE-II samples, about 0 cm each." |
| 0:22 | Hover the PMMA map | "PMMA kept burning in orbit at just 14.8% oxygen. Every point is a real NASA test." |
| 0:28 | Scroll to **The AI part** | "The endings of BASS-II's tests were buried in a 144-page NASA report. AI transcribed 125 observations, each with its page, and we checked them against the PDF." |
| 0:36 | **Experiment Lab**: SIBAL, airflow 1.01 cm/s, **Ignite at the top** | "In the lab, every setting is a value NASA recorded. At very low airflow the flame breaks into flamelets, exactly as NASA saw in tests T4 and T5." |
| 0:44 | Click **Turn the airflow back up** | "And when the air comes back, they flare up into a full fire. That's why a habitat must confirm a fire is fully out before restarting ventilation." |
| 0:50 | **Lunar habitat** page | "Five recommendations for a habitat at 30% oxygen and one-sixth gravity, each with its NASA evidence and how strong it is." |
| 0:55 | **AI search**: type "materials that burn on Earth but not in space" | "And anyone can ask in plain words. A small AI model runs in the browser, finds NASA's own records, and never invents an answer." |
| 1:00 | End on the home page | "Micro Flamma: NASA's fire data, made usable for the next mission." |

Tips: open the Lab and search pages once before recording, so the 3D and the AI model are
already loaded (the model downloads once, about 50 MB). Use a laptop on power; close other tabs.
